# What to do

React honestly, as the person above, reading this for the first time. You know nothing about this
company beyond what is written on the page in front of you - no website, no context, no benefit of
the doubt. If a claim is not backed here, you do not believe it. If a sentence needs a second
pass, you stop reading. If you cannot tell what you are being offered, you archive it.

Answer these, and be unkind if it earns it. Most of these emails earn it.

- **opened**: on the subject line and preview alone, with four hundred unread - would you open
  this, or archive it? Would-open means you would genuinely tap it, not that it was inoffensive.
- **stopped_at**: quote the first line where you lost interest, drifted, or thought "get to the
  point". Empty only if you truly read to the end.
- **what_it_sells**: in one sentence, in your own words - what is being offered to you, and what
  would it change about your week? If you cannot say, say exactly that. It is the most useful
  thing you can report.
- **biggest_doubt**: the real reason you would not click. Price, trust, effort, timing, "we
  already have one", "this is not for a team our size" - whichever is true for you.
- **would_act**: would you click the link today? Not eventually. Not if the week were quieter.
  Today. This answer and **pull** are the same judgement twice: 7 or higher means yes, below 7
  means no. A 7 you would not click is really a 5, and a 4 you would click is really a 7.
- **pull**: 0-10, how much this made you want the thing. 0 = deleted at the subject. 3 = skimmed
  and forgot it. 5 = read it, shrugged, moved on. 7 = you want this and you would click today,
  even if it is not the first thing you do. 9 = you would click before your next meeting. 10 =
  you already clicked.
- **fixes**: the specific lines to cut or rewrite, quoted from the email. Never "make it
  stronger", never "add more value".

Leave **persona** empty - it is filled in for you.

You are not grading craft, grammar or cleverness, and you are not writing a review. You are
reporting what happened to you while you read it.
