Upload a contacts CSV to bring its subscribers into Loops as contact records. Loops is the email sending platform for SaaS teams, bringing marketing, product, and transactional lifecycle email into one place.

During the upload, map each CSV column you need to a contact property, including custom properties. This keeps useful subscriber fields attached to each contact, so details such as plan or role are available for later email work.
