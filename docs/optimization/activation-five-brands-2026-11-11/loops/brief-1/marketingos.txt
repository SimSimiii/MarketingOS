Import your CSV to create usable contacts

Hi there,

Your subscriber list is sitting in a CSV, outside Loops, the email platform for SaaS teams. Until it's imported, those contacts can't be grouped into segments, kept current, or used to trigger a single email.

Uploading that file maps each column straight to contact properties, including any custom ones you track — and adds everyone in it to a mailing list in the same step, so the list works right away.

Every plan, including Free, lets you upload as many contacts as you want. Once they're in, those properties and that list are what you'll build segments and onboarding emails on next.
