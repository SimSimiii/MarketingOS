Turn your website into a support channel

Hi there,

Put Customerly live chat on your website so customers have a direct place to start a support conversation.

The documented first step is specific: insert the provided Customerly script into your website’s `<head>` section. This installs the live-chat widget on your site.

Customerly is an AI helpdesk where the widget supports Aura answering first, with human agents able to take over conversations with context when needed. Your team stays part of the conversation.

The live-chat documentation walks you through the installation path for adding the widget to your website.

Set up live chat → https://docs.customerly.io/en/live-chat
