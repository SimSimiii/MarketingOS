Hi there,

Start with Typefully’s post preview before you schedule. Typefully is a social media scheduler, and its preview lets you check threads, mentions, and media before a post goes live.

Use that check to confirm the post you’re about to place on your calendar. You can see what’s scheduled, in review, and ready to ship.

Once the preview looks right, choose one specific publication date and time for the post, then schedule it in Typefully. That gives the post a clear place on your calendar and completes the scheduling step.
