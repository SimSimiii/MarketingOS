Give your rough notes a first shape

Hi there,

Rough notes are enough to begin. Start a new Typefully draft and add the fragments, points, or half-formed sentences you want to develop.

Typefully is a focused social-post writing workspace. Once your notes are in the draft, its AI writing tools can expand or rewrite them, giving you another way to shape the first version.

If the substance is already there, you don’t need a different topic. You need a place to start turning it into a post.

The first draft doesn’t need to be perfect. It just needs to begin.
