What they are doing: Shared inbox triage of rising ticket volume; wants AI to absorb repetitive simple questions so humans handle complex/high-value conversations without a disruptive tool swap

What they need: Extend support capacity during a volume spike or headcount loss without adding staff or forcing the team through a new, disruptive support stack

User: Support agents and team lead

Decision maker: Head of Support / Support Team Lead

Current workaround: Status quo shared inbox (Help Scout, Zendesk, or email) stretched thinner by remaining staff, or a bolt-on AI chatbot vendor requiring a separate rollout

Relevance hypothesis: Vendor explicitly targets this buyer with AI Answers positioned as a low-disruption capacity extension; market reviews confirm 'ease over scale' as the reason this cohort chooses Help Scout, with a known scale ceiling

Proposed trigger: Loss of a team member or rising conversation volume outpacing current staff
