A scheduling link can follow rules, not just expose open slots. SavvyCal is online meeting scheduling software that lets you set those rules around link-booked calls.

Minimum Notice requires lead time before a new event. Time Blocking lets SavvyCal honor calendar events you use to budget your time.

Buffers & Limits can also cap meeting quantity by day, week, or month. Your calendar gets a vote before another call lands on it.

An account gives you a place to try these controls against the boundaries you want for client, partner, and investor calls—and decide whether they fit your week.
