You can inspect Plausible Analytics’ live dashboard before deciding whether it belongs in a formal privacy and reporting assessment. It shows where traffic comes from, which pages are being viewed and which goals are being met.

Plausible is a web analytics service that uses no cookies and collects no personal data. It also uses no persistent identifiers or cross-site or cross-device tracking, and does not reuse site data for other purposes.

The public demo provides a working view of its core reporting without requiring an account. Your team can compare the dashboard with its own requirements before committing time to a fuller assessment.

It is a concrete way to judge whether cookie-free analytics can provide the basic traffic, page and goal information your services need.
