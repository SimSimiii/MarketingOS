Make customer-facing forms feel like yours

Hi there,

Customer-facing forms should feel like they belong to the company behind them. With Tally Pro, your team can maintain intake and survey forms together, then present them without Tally branding and under your own branded link.

Tally is an online form builder. Its Pro plan lets you invite unlimited team members to shared workspaces, giving operations and customer success one shared place to maintain the forms customers see.

The same Pro plan removes Tally branding and hosts forms on a custom subdomain. Once that subdomain is pointed at Tally, you can use branded form links that keep the experience under your company’s name.

The official pricing page lays out these Pro capabilities in one place, so you can decide whether its collaboration and branding controls fit the way your team wants to run customer-facing forms.
