When a new client or internal podcast needs a host, you can place it in the same Transistor account as your other shows. A paid account does not add a separate charge for creating each additional podcast.

Use that account for the podcasts your agency produces, then publish each one to the listening apps its audience uses.

The free trial runs for 14 days, giving you a defined window to decide whether Transistor fits the hosting plan for your next show.
