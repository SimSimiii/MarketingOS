Hi there,

Start with a source Autopilot supports: Intercom, Zendesk, Freshdesk, Gong or Zoom. Once connected, it automatically captures feedback from customer chats, sales calls and reviews.

Canny is customer feedback management software that centralizes captured feedback. Autopilot reads each conversation, ticket or review, extracts individual feature requests and merges detected duplicates.

The resulting feedback appears in Canny, where Autopilot triages it to the right team. You can see the extracted requests and merged feedback without working through each source separately.

Autopilot is included in every Canny plan, including Free. Create your Free account, choose a supported source and see how its feedback is captured, deduplicated and triaged.
