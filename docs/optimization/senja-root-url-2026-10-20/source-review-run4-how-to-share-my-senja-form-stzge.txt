# How to share my Senja form

 

Once your form is ready, it's time to get it in front of your customers. Every sharing option lives in one place: the **Share** window of your form. This article walks through each one, and helps you pick where to start.

This is applicable for all Senja plans: Free, Starter, and Pro.

## Open the Share window

- Go to your **Forms** page: <https://app.senja.io/forms>
- Click the ⚙️ icon on your form and choose **Share**

  ![](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01KXRD01NCX49K8W83NB6MPBG0.png)

## Copy your form link

In the **Share** window, copy the form link.

![](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01KXRD0SRPB11PVQAK1DE6XBTW.png) 

You can also copy it straight from your **Forms** page:

- **List view**: click the 🔗 icon
- **Expanded view**: click the link under the form's name

  ![](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01KTVK6DJPSWAC1BJYXJ9M1FT8.png)

Paste your link anywhere your customers are:

- Newsletters and marketing emails
- Slack or community channels
- Your email signature
- Direct messages to customers

Want a cleaner link? Change the end of it in your form's **Settings** tab, see [How to set up the form settings](/how-to-set-up-the-form-settings-amayq).

## Share on social media or messaging apps

Post your form on **X**, **Facebook**, **LinkedIn**, **WhatsApp**, and more.

- In the **Share** window, choose the channel you want

  ![](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01KXRE42FFPH4GRA4WSXHYC45X.png)
- Edit the post if you like, then publish it

Using a channel that isn't listed? Copy your form link and paste it into your post or message.

## Download a QR code

Meeting customers in person, at a conference, over coffee, or at an event? A QR code is the quickest way to share your form.

- In the **Share** window, click **Download** under **Share your form's QR code**

  ![](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01KXREBGQPENJTJ3RBKWQ6Y23P.png)
- Print it, add it to your slides, or show it on your phone

## Embed on your website

Put your form right on your website so visitors can leave a testimonial without leaving the page. Each form has its own embed code.

- In the **Share** window, click the **Embed** tab
- Copy the code under **Your Code Snippet**

  ![](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01M4T67W1D8XXH5RTJE9W1GW17.png)
- Paste it into an HTML or embed element on your website

**From the Chrome extension**

With Senja's free [Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/senja-testimonial-extensi/lmldakdjelendidnmaieidggbffljlle?hl=en), you can grab the embed code without opening Senja.

- Open the page where you want to embed the form
- Click the 💜 Senja icon in your extension toolbar and choose **Forms**
- Find your form and click **Copy Embed**

  ![](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01M4T7A4Y6SPZ55TBXM78NS6YH.png)

## Add to your course

Ask students for a testimonial right inside a lesson. Adding it partway through the course, not only at the end, usually gets you more testimonials.

- In the **Share** window, click the **Add to Course** tab
- Copy the code under **Your Code Snippet**

  ![](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01KXRENNQE0APCHQ8XJTCV95QK.png)
- Paste it into a lesson on your course platform

Step-by-step guides for Kajabi, Teachable, Thinkific, Podia, Circle, LearnDash, LearnWorlds, and Skool are in [Collect testimonials in your course](https://support.senja.io/collect-testimonials-in-your-course-rta3p).

## Send it by email from Senja

You don't have to share your link yourself. Senja can email your customers for you:

- **Form invites**: send a personal request to one customer or a whole list, and track who opened, clicked, and submitted. See [How do I use form invites?](/how-do-i-use-form-invites-5kosu)
- **Auto-collect**: send requests automatically when a customer buys, finishes a course, or gets tagged in your tools (paid plans only). See [How to collect testimonials automatically with form](/how-to-send-testimonial-requests-automatically-9kdf1)

## Which method should you start with?

It depends on your business:

- **Course platforms**: add your form to a lesson, or automate a request when a student finishes the course
- **E-commerce**: automate a request after each purchase
- **Service businesses**: send a form invite when you wrap up a project
- **SaaS**: automate requests when customers reach a usage milestone
- **Events and in-person work**: use your QR code

Start with one method, then add more as you build momentum. For timing ideas, see [When to ask for testimonials](internal://art_01JX37CR5SVD05KFDZS529RM1Q).
