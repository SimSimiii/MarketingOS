# How to set up questions and pages in Senja form

 

The **Form** tab is where you build what your customers see: the welcome page, your questions, the details you collect about them, and the thank you page. This article covers everything you can do there.

The Form tab is available on all Senja plans: Free, Starter, and Pro. Welcome videos need a paid plan.

## Open the Form tab

- Go to your **Forms** page: <https://app.senja.io/forms>
- Click the ⚙️ icon on your form and choose **Edit**
- Click the **Form** tab
- Scroll through the slides, or click one to edit it in the right sidebar
- Click **Save changes** when you’re done

## Edit the text on any slide

Every slide comes with example copy you can change:

- **Page title**: the question or heading
- **Subtitle**: the line under the title
- **Placeholder**: example text inside the answer box
- **Call to action**: the text on the button

You can format text with bold, italics, bullets, and numbered lists using the built-in editor.

When you edit a question, the change also shows on past responses to that question. If you want a new question without changing old answers, add a new question instead of editing the existing one.

## Add questions

- Scroll to **Question #1** and click **Add a question**
- Pick from the directory of 50+ proven testimonial questions (you can search by business type), or write your own
- Choose a question type: text, video, or rating
- Drag questions to reorder them

Need inspiration? See the [testimonial questions directory](https://senja.io/testimonial-questions) on our blog.

### Collect video only, text only, or both

Each question has its own setting in the right sidebar:

- **Video**: customers can only record or upload a video
- **Text**: customers can only write
- **Video and text**: customers pick one format for that question

Customers can’t submit both a video and text for the same question. If you want both, you have three options:

- Collect video only and use the transcript, since Senja transcribes every video
- Add two questions, one set to video and one to text
- Create a separate form for each format

Ask customers to record in one orientation so your widgets look consistent. Portrait (4:5) at 1080p or higher works best, with their face centred. You can put this tip in your question subtitle.

### Ask for multiple videos

Add more than one question and set each to **Video**. You can also mix video and text questions in the same form.

On your **Proof** page, all of a customer’s answers appear together in one entry.

When there are several videos, Senja also creates a multiclip, which plays them back as one video. See [What can I do with the multiclip videos in my Proof page?](/what-can-i-do-with-the-multiclip-videos-in-my-proof-page-2tzcg)

### Your primary question

The first question in a new form is your **primary question**. Senja sets this automatically and it stays that way:

- You can edit its text, but you can’t delete it
- It keeps the primary label even if you drag other questions above it
- You can’t move the label to another question
- You can spot it in the Form tab because it’s the only question without a 🗑️ icon

By default, widgets and Walls of Love show answers to the primary question. To show answers to your other questions, see [How to choose testimonials from multipart responses for the widget](/how-to-choose-testimonials-from-multipart-responses-for-the-widget-a19fg).

Make your primary question the one you most want to show on your website.

### Delete or hide questions

- Scroll to the question you want to remove
- Click the 🗑️ icon in the top right corner of the slide
- Click **Delete** to confirm

Deleting a question doesn’t delete the answers you already collected. They stay on your **Proof** page, marked as belonging to a deleted question.

You can hide or delete any question or page except the primary question, **About you** **page**, and **Thank you & word of mouth page**.

### Required and optional answers

Customers have to answer every question you show. There’s no option to make a question optional yet. If you’d like this, [add it to our feedback board](https://feedback.senja.io/) so others can upvote it.

The **About you** page is the only place you can choose what’s required. **Your name** is always required. You can turn other fields on or off, and click the down arrow next to a field to make it required or optional.

### Add a welcome video

Welcome videos need a paid plan: Starter or Pro.

A short video of you explaining why their testimonial matters makes the request feel personal, and more people finish the form.

- Scroll to the **Welcome page** slide
- Click **Record a welcome video**
- Turn off **Fit video to screen** if you want to show the video at its original size
- Click **Save changes**

You can add a video to the **Thank you & word of mouth** page the same way.

If you want to remove the welcome video, click **Remove welcome video** on the right side bar.

### The other pages in your form

- **About you** and **About company**: collect your customer’s details, add custom fields, or pass data in with hidden fields. See [How do I collect custom information from my customers?](/how-do-i-collect-custom-information-from-my-customers-4mvlk)
- **Consent page**: on by default, so customers can choose public or private use. See [How do I collect consent to use testimonials from my customers?](/how-to-collect-consent-to-use-testimonials-6yx6m)
- **Thank you & word of mouth page**: the last screen customers see, where you can add a call to action or let them share their testimonial. See [How do I edit my form thank you page?](https://support.senja.io/how-do-i-edit-my-form-thank-you-page-c61sq)

### Change how your form looks

Click the **Design** subtab inside the Form tab to change your logo, colors, fonts, layout, and button style. See [How do I customize my form design?](/how-do-i-customize-my-form-design-xscki)
