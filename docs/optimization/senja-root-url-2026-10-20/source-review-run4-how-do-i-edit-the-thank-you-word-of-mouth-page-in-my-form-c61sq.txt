# How do I edit the Thank you & word of mouth page in my form?

 

The **Thank you & word of mouth page** is the last screen customers see in your form, right after they submit their testimonial. You can change the message, add a video, send them to another page, or invite them to share their testimonial.

This is available on all Senja plans: Free, Starter, and Pro. Thank you videos, custom calls to action, and social sharing need a paid plan (Starter or Pro).

## Not the same as Thank Yous

Senja has three things with "thank you" in the name. This article covers the first one.

| Feature | What it is | Where to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Thank you & word of mouth page** | The screen customers see in your form after they submit | Form editor → **Form** tab |
| **Confirmation email** | The automatic email customers get after they submit, which also delivers any reward | Form editor → **Settings** tab → **Send confirmation email** |
| **Thank Yous** | A separate feature for sending personal thank you emails to any customer, at any time, with a video, coupon, or gift | **Thank Yous** in the left sidebar. See [How do I send thank you notes to my customers?](/how-do-i-send-thank-you-notes-to-my-customers-h9rcf) |

## Edit the page

- Go to your **Forms** page: <https://app.senja.io/forms>
- Click the ⚙️ icon on your form and choose **Edit**
- Go to the **Form** tab and scroll down to the **Thank you & word of mouth page**
- Update the text in the right sidebar
- Click **Save changes**

  ![](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01KTVHRDSPDAP4BD3D1NXKSXE5.png)

If your form has a reward, customers see it on this page too. To set one up, see [How to reward customers for testimonials](/how-to-reward-customers-for-testimonials-6z0qn).

## Add a thank you video

Thank you videos need a paid plan: Starter or Pro.

A short video from you makes the thank you feel personal.

- On the **Thank you & word of mouth page**, click **Record a welcome video**
- Record your video or upload one
- Click **Save changes**

## Send customers to another page

Custom calls to action need a paid plan: Starter or Pro.

Add a button that takes customers somewhere else once they're done, like a product page, your booking page, or a review site where you'd like them to leave a review too.

- On the **Thank you & word of mouth page**, turn on **Add a custom call to action**
- Write the button text in **Call to action**
- Paste the link in **Call to action URL**

  ![](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01M0ZPBXT61ZSYEDTMCPHXJMVJ.png)
- Click **Save changes**

## Let customers share their testimonial

Social sharing needs a paid plan: Starter or Pro.

Customers can post their testimonial on social media in one click, with a link back to your website. That's the word of mouth part of the page.

- On the **Thank you & word of mouth page**, turn on **Allow people to share their testimonials**
- Choose which network to feature in **Highlight sharing on...**
- Add **Your website URL**

  ![](https://static.ferndesk.com/user-images/file_01M0ZPQ95ZSEZK96PQ0YKQ8RNT.png)
- Click **Save changes**

When customers click **Post in one click**, the social network opens with their testimonial and your website link ready to post. More details in [Can I ask my customers to share their testimonial?](/can-i-ask-my-customers-to-share-their-testimonial-syyuv)
