# Senja forms overview

 

Senja forms are personalised pages where your customers can leave you a text or video testimonial in about 30 seconds. They don’t need an account or any special software. This article covers what forms can do, with links to the detailed guides for each part.

Forms are available on all Senja plans: Free, Starter, and Pro. Some features need a paid plan, and they’re marked below.

## How many forms you can have

Your limit counts the forms currently on your dashboard, not every form you’ve ever created.

- **Free** plan: 1 form
- **Starter** plan: 3 forms
- **Pro** plan: unlimited forms

Deleting a form frees up a slot right away. Deleted forms can’t be restored.

### Why use more than one form

Different parts of your business deserve different questions. A separate form for each one gets you testimonials that are more specific, and more useful wherever you show them.

Each form has its own link, so you can send the right one to the right people.

- **One form per product or service**: so each product page shows social proof about that product
- **Customer support**: ask customers about the help they got from your team
- **Employee testimonials**: collect stories from your team for your careers page
- **Case studies**: gather in-depth write-ups from your most successful customers

## Create your form

- Go to your **Forms** page: <https://app.senja.io/forms>
- Click **+ Create new**
- Start from **Example form** or pick a template (SaaS, courses, and more)
- Click **Save changes** before moving between tabs

A form has three main tabs:

- **Reward**: offer a gift to customers who submit a testimonial
- **Form**: write your questions and change the design
- **Settings**: slug, notifications, auto-approval, tags, translation, and more

## Choose what to ask

- Add as many questions as you need with **Add a question**, and drag to reorder them
- Question types include text, video, and rating
- Limit a form to video testimonials only or text testimonials only
- Ask for multiple videos in one form
- Collect extra details like company size or role with [additional fields](/how-do-i-collect-custom-information-from-my-customers-4mvlk) on the **About you** or **About company page**

## Make it look like your brand

In the **Form** tab, open the **Design** subtab to change your logo, primary color, fonts, page layout, and button style.

You can also edit all the text, add a spokesperson avatar, and change the thank you email copy. Changes show in the preview straight away.

Removing Senja branding needs a paid plan.

## Useful settings

In the **Settings** tab you can:

- Change the **Form slug** (the end of your form URL)
- Get **Slack notifications** for new submissions
- **Approve testimonials** automatically
- Ask customers to record in landscape on mobile
- **Tag collected testimonials** automatically
- **Translate collected testimonials** into your main language (Pro plan only, [details here](/how-to-set-up-the-form-settings-amayq))
- **Send confirmation email** to thank customers and deliver their reward
- **Stop collecting new submissions** to pause the form

Form questions themselves can’t be auto-translated. You’ll need to write them in the language you want.

## After someone submits

- Every form includes a consent message by default ([more on consent](/how-to-collect-consent-to-use-testimonials-6yx6m))
- Customise the **Thank you & word of mouth** page, or send customers to an external page with your own call to action ([how to edit it](/how-do-i-edit-the-thank-you-word-of-mouth-page-in-my-form-c61sq))
- Let customers [share their testimonial on social media](/can-i-ask-my-customers-to-share-their-testimonial-syyuv) straight after submitting

## Share your form

There are four ways to get your form in front of customers.

| Action | On List view | On Expanded view |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copy and share the form link([details](/how-to-share-my-senja-form-stzge)) | Click the 🔗 icon | Click the link under the form’s name |
| Send form invites from Senja to emails from your existing list, a CSV/XLS file, another platform, or Google contacts([details](/how-do-i-use-form-invites-5kosu)) | Click the ⚙️ icon, choose **Invites** | Click **Invites** button |
| Embed the form ([details](/how-to-share-my-senja-form-stzge)) | Click the ⚙️ icon, choose **Share**, then the **Embed** tab, and paste the code into your website | Click the ⚙️ icon, choose **Share**, then the **Embed** tab, and paste the code into your website |

**You can also put your form inside your course or community.** We have guides for [Kajabi](/collect-testimonials-in-kajabi-pgx3i), [Teachable](/collect-testimonials-in-teachable-gcxkf), [Thinkific](/collect-testimonials-in-thinkific-87msn), [Podia](/collect-testimonials-in-podia-7t99i), [Circle](/collect-testimonials-in-circle-p9sah), [LearnDash](/collect-testimonials-in-learndash-43a5c), [LearnWorlds](/collect-testimonials-in-learnworlds-g0yi9), and [Skool](/collect-testimonials-in-skool-wnvnl).

## Collect on autopilot

Auto-collect needs a paid plan: Starter or Pro.

Connect your form to the tools you already use, and Senja sends a testimonial request when a customer does something there, like buying, finishing a course, or getting tagged in your CRM. Follow-up reminders stop as soon as they submit.

To set it up, click **Invites** on your form and go to the **Automate** tab. For Stripe, use the main **Invites** tab.

Supported platforms include Shopify, Stripe, Kajabi, Teachable, Thinkific, Intercom, Help Scout, Kit, MailerLite, and more.

Full list and steps: [How to collect testimonials automatically with form](/how-to-send-testimonial-requests-automatically-9kdf1)
