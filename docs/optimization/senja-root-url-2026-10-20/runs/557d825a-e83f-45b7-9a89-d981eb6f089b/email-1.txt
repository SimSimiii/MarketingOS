With Senja, you can create a student video testimonial request before your next cohort launch.

Start with a concrete prompt: ask students to describe their starting point and what changed during the program. That gives you a specific request to try, with room for students to tell their own stories.

The free tier lets you collect up to 15 video or text testimonials. You can evaluate the form and invitation workflow before committing to a paid plan.
