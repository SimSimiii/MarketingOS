Senja’s testimonial collection software gives your students two ways to provide video proof for your next cohort launch.

You create a simple or multistep collection form. A student sharing their course experience can record a video directly in that form or upload one they’ve already recorded.

Students don’t need an account or special software to submit through a Senja form. That makes the recording choice a concrete place to start evaluating how you’d collect on-camera feedback from a cohort.

Senja’s official collection guide documents both options, so you can inspect that part of the process before deciding whether to try a collection form.
