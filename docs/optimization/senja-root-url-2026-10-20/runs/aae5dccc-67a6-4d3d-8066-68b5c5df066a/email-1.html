Students can submit a video without creating an account.

Senja is testimonial collection software for requesting student videos and displaying the responses. For a future cohort launch, the collection starts with a form you share by link.

After signup, the Free plan includes one form and collection of up to 15 video and text testimonials. You can start from a course template, set a question to Video, and copy the form’s link to share with students.

A student follows that link to record or upload a video through the form. They don’t need an account or special software.

Once a student submits a video, Senja automatically transcribes it for viewing as text. That same video can be used in a website widget—so you can check both the transcript and a display option for the submitted testimony.
