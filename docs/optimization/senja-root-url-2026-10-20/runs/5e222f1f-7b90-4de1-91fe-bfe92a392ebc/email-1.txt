Senja lets you build a student testimonial collection form for your next cohort launch.

It’s testimonial collection software for gathering video and text feedback. After creating a free account, you can build a simple or multistep form for your course, then check whether the request covers the student story you want prospective students to hear.

The invitation workflow lets you invite students individually or in bulk and send follow-ups. You can evaluate the form before sending your first request.

The free allowance covers up to 15 video or text testimonials. That gives you a bounded way to try collecting student proof before deciding whether Senja fits your next launch.
