Hi there,

Senja collects text and video testimonials, and the student's side of it is one page you build yourself first.

In a free account you write the questions you want to put to your cohort and set each one to text, video or a rating. A whole form can be limited to video only, or to text only.

What your student opens is a personalised page. They write or record their answer there, with no account and no special software — Senja describes that as taking about 30 seconds.

Worth knowing before you send it: every question you show has to be answered, there's no optional question yet, and their name is always required.

Then the chasing is a switch rather than a habit. Turn follow-ups on and Senja sends up to three reminder emails, 1, 3 and 7 days after the invite, only to students who haven't responded. Three is the maximum.

Build it, open the page from your student's side, and decide then whether it's a request you'd be comfortable sending.
