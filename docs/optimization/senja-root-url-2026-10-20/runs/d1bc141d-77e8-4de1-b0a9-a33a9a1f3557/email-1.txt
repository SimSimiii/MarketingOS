Subject: One form, three reminders, set once
Preview text: Build the testimonial ask in Senja instead of messaging each quiet student yourself.

Hi there,

Senja collects text and video testimonials, and the part worth your attention is the asking: you build one collection form, switch follow-ups on, then send it to your current or most recent cohort.

Senja reckons a simple or multistep form takes about 60 seconds to build. From then on the chasing is bounded — up to three reminder emails, at 1, 3 and 7 days after the invite, and only to students who haven't answered.

Video is one option, not the price of entry. Your student opens a personalised page and leaves a text or video answer, with no account to create and nothing to install. Senja puts that at about 30 seconds.

Reminders only go out on invites you send after switching them on, so the form comes first. A free account is where it gets built: the questions, the page your cohort will open, the follow-up schedule, all before anything is sent.

The free plan holds 15 text and video testimonials, and new ones past that stay hidden until you upgrade.
