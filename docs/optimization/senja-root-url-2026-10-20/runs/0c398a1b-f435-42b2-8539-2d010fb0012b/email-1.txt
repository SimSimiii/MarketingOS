You set the format of the answer before you send the ask.

Senja collects text and video testimonials from customers, and the format is a per-question setting: set a question to Video and a student has to record or upload one, on the page itself, with no account to create.

Your last cohort comes in from an existing mailing list or a CSV, invited individually or in bulk.

Switch on up to three follow-ups while you build the invite — they apply only to invites sent after that, and land 1, 3 and 7 days later, to students who haven't answered yet.

Invites run on any plan, Free included, and so does video collection. Before anything goes out, you can see the recipient list, how many reminders exist, the dates they land, and the page a student is asked to fill in.
