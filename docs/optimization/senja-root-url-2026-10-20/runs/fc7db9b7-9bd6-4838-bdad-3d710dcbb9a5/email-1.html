A student video request doesn’t need a paid plan.

Senja is testimonial collection software for requesting student feedback through personalised forms. Students can submit a video without an account or special software. You can invite them individually or in bulk.

Free includes form invitations and collection of 15 testimonials total, across video and text. That’s a testimonial allowance, not an invitation count. Beyond that allowance, new testimonials are hidden until you upgrade.

Follow-ups are your choice. Senja supports up to three optional reminders for students who haven’t responded, scheduled 1, 3 and 7 days after the original invitation.

A free account gives you a starting point for evaluating one student video request and whether that reminder schedule fits your cohort.
