Senja’s Free plan includes a form for collecting student video testimonials.

Senja is testimonial collection software. You share a personalised form; students can submit a video without creating an account or installing special software.

Free includes one form and 15 testimonials across video and text. After that limit, new testimonials are hidden until you upgrade. The form keeps working.

Separately, Senja supports individual invitations and optional reminders: up to three emails to nonresponders, sent 1, 3 and 7 days after the original invite. Those capabilities aren’t confirmed here as Free-plan inclusions.
