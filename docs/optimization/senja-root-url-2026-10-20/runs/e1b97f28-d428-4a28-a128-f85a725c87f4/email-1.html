Hi there,

Students don’t need a Senja account to submit a video.

Senja is testimonial collection software: students can record or upload their video through a form, without an account or special software.

After signing up, you can set a form question’s answer type to Video to request an on-camera response about their course experience.

Once a student supplies a video, Senja automatically creates a transcript you can read as text. If a course sales page is your intended destination, you can also display the video in a website widget without coding, with permission to publish.

That gives you a concrete evaluation: how the submitted testimony reads as text and works on the page.
