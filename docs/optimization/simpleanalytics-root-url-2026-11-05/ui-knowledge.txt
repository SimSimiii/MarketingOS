objection
56/100

They say no because: "Your cookieless count is incomplete too — ad blockers, JavaScript off, bots, preview domains. You're selling me a different flavour of wrong.". Answered by: Partly answered, and the limits are documented rather than hidden. A custom domain on your own subdomain is offered specifically to bypass ad blockers; a noscript tag captures visitors with JavaScript disabled; robot blocking and one-click referral-spam removal exist; office IPs can be excluded and there is an extension to block your own visits. Against that: the 2–10% ad-blocker range is the vendor's own unattributed estimate, data from shared preview and proxy domains like *.vercel.app is dropped unless the exact hostname is added, the noscript tag may increase bot counts, and the vendor states Simple Analytics can also miss events. The defensible claim is 'observed, independent, and its gaps are known', not 'complete'. [E18, E156, E38, E157, E158, E26, E152, E19, E43, E54]
