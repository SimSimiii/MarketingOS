An August-to-July diary, made personal

Hi there,

Papier’s academic-year diary runs from August through July, with a week-to-view or day-to-view layout. It gives the academic calendar a paper home you can make your own.

Choose a hardback designed to fit comfortably into most bags, or a larger spiral with more room to write. Add your name and, if you like, a custom diary title.

Inside, weekly overviews, monthly spreads and term timetables sit alongside deadline and grade trackers, with dedicated space for goals, notes, gratitude and reflection. Practical plans and personal pages stay together across the same academic year.

The Papier guide explains the August-to-July format and its planning sections, so you can decide whether that rhythm belongs on your desk.
