Hi there,

Before evaluating analytics for a client site, you can review the exact data footprint the service is designed to use.

Simple Analytics is cookie-free website analytics. It uses no persistent identifiers, stores no IP addresses—they are discarded immediately—and does not link its metrics to identifiable users.

Its public data-processing page lists the scope: Page URL, referrer, UTM parameters, time zone, and device and browser type. You can inspect these fields without signing up or installing anything.

This gives you a concrete model to review and explain, rather than a general privacy claim. Compare the documented fields with each client’s requirements, then decide whether Simple Analytics is suitable for an evaluation on their site.

Review the data scope → https://www.simpleanalytics.com/data-processing-agreement
