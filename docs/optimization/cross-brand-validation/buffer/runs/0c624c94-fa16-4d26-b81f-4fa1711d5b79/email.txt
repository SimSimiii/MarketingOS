Subject: When it’s time to plan next week’s posts
Preview text: If you use Insights, compare engagement rates before choosing the next batch.

Hi there,

A weekly test can stay small: schedule a few posts, let them publish, then use the results when choosing the next batch.

Buffer is a social media workspace for scheduling posts across connected channels and checking how they performed. On the Free plan, you can keep up to 10 posts scheduled per channel; each published post opens that slot again.

If you include Insights in the test, its Performance per Post table sorts published content by engagement rate. That gives you a concrete next step: decide which posts are worth repeating or building on when planning the following week. Free Insights also includes a Last 30 days view.

The official Insights guide shows how that performance comparison works. It won’t determine which metrics your particular channels report, but it can help you judge whether this review step is useful before signing up.
