Queue this week’s posts, then review their performance

Hi there,

Buffer lets you queue a few posts across connected social channels, then review their performance in Insights. It’s social media management software for scheduling posts and inspecting the results in one place.

At the start of the week, add posts to each channel’s queue. After they publish, check recent engagement, impressions, and post performance before planning your next batch.

The Free forever plan supports up to three channels and 10 scheduled posts per channel at one time. Insights includes 30 days of history, so you can compare those limits with the routine you have in mind.

Check Buffer’s official plans to decide whether the Free entry fits your week.
