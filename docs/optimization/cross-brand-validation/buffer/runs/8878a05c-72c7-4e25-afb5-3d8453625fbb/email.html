Planning ahead can still leave room for what happens locally.

Buffer is a social media workspace for scheduling posts across connected channels and checking how they performed. Queue a few posts at the start of the week, then drag any post to a new slot when a timely update belongs there.

After they publish, check available engagement, impressions, and individual post performance before planning the next batch. Foster Coffee Company found that two weeks ahead was its sweet spot while still moving posts and adding timely shop content.

Free supports up to three connected channels and 10 scheduled posts per channel at one time. Essentials starts at $5/month for one channel when billed yearly. Paid scheduling is described as unlimited, with a fair-use cap of 5,000 posts per channel.
