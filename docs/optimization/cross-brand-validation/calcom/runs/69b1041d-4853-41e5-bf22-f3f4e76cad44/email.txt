A discovery-call path can begin with one relevant question. The answer can determine which consultant receives the booking.

Cal.com is scheduling software that combines client-intake questions with consultant-routing rules. A routing form could ask about the prospect’s challenge, industry, or language preference, then use that response to assign the consultant responsible for that need.

Questions, answer options, and routing logic can be adjusted with the no-code form builder as offerings change.

The official consulting and coaching routing guide demonstrates the full path: capture a need before booking, apply an attribute-based rule, and route the discovery call to the relevant consultant. It provides a concrete way to assess whether that mechanism represents one of your team’s call paths.
