Hi there,

You can see which sources and landing pages drive your trial signups — not just how many there are.

That's what Plausible Analytics does. It's a privacy-friendly web analytics dashboard, and once you set up a conversion goal for your trial signup, a raw count turns into the referrer sources and entry pages that produced it.

You might expect that to mean editing your site's code. The simplest goal doesn't. A pageview goal watches the page a visitor lands on right after registering, and pageviews are already collected — so no code changes.

Once it's set and the first conversion records, the goal shows up in the Goals section of your dashboard. Click it, and the signups break down by source and entry page.

The official goal-conversions guide walks through both goal types and what each one reports — a good place to set up a new goal or review one you already have.
