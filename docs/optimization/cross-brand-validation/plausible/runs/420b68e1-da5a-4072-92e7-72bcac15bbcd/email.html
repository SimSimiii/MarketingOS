After a signup burst, the number you judge it by is the raw total.

That total counts whatever reached the page — bots, referrer spam and data-center traffic included. So the conversion rate you work out from it may not reflect real people.

Plausible is a privacy-friendly web analytics dashboard. It excludes bots, referrer spam and data-center traffic out of the box, so the visits it counts are real human ones.

Set your trial signup as a goal, and the dashboard reports that goal's conversion rate against those filtered visits — along with the referrer sources and entry pages driving each signup. One total becomes a breakdown of where real signups come from.

The official goal-conversions guide walks through setting up a signup goal, or reviewing one you already have. That is the concrete first step before any of it shows up for your own site.
