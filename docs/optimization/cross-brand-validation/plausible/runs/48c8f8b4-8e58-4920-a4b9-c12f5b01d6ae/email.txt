Subject: Your signup count can't tell you where they came from
Preview text: Mark the signup as a goal and Plausible reports the sources and entry pages behind each one.

Hi there,

In Plausible, you can mark your trial signup as a goal and then see which pages and sources are actually producing it.

A signup count on its own carries no breakdown. The number rises and falls, but it can't tell you whether a particular landing page or channel did the work or just happened to move at the same time.

For a register or confirmation page, that's a pageview goal — no code changes, since Plausible collects pageviews automatically.

Once it records its first conversion, the Goals section reports that goal's conversion rate, the referrer sources and entry pages driving those conversions, and the top pages people convert on.

The official goal-conversions guide walks through setting one up — pageview or custom event — and reading what the dashboard shows about it. It's the concrete first step from one signup total to signups you can trace back.
