Subject: Which referrers are tied to completed trial signups?
Preview text: With a distinct confirmation URL, a pageview goal can also show entry pages.

Hi there,

Plausible Analytics can track completed trial signups as a goal, then show which referrer sources and entry pages are associated with those conversions.

First, check whether a successful signup reaches a distinct confirmation URL. Once Plausible is collecting pageviews on the site, that URL can become a pageview goal without another code change.

After conversions are recorded, filtering the dashboard by that goal can show its conversion count and rate, referrer sources, entry pages and the top pages where people convert.

Inspect the official goal-conversions guide to decide whether a pageview goal fits your signup flow.
