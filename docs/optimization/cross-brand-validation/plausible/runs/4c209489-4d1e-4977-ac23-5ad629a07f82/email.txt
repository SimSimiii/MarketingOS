Subject: The signup that loads no new page
Preview text: Why a page-based goal can miss the conversions you most want to count

Hi there,

Most signup tracking keys on a page load — the thank-you screen, the confirmation URL that appears when someone finishes.

If your signup completes inside the app without ever loading a distinct page, that approach never records it. You end up undercounting exactly the signups you're trying to judge.

Plausible is a privacy-friendly analytics dashboard with two goal types. A pageview goal matches a URL. A custom event goal captures the signup as an event sent the moment it fires — through a small snippet or the Events API.

Yes, that event needs a line of code. Once it's in place, the goal reports alongside your traffic sources and entry pages, so you can see which ones bring in real signups.

The official goal-conversions guide walks through how both goal types are set up and what the Goals section reports. Read it and you can pick the one that fits how your own signup flow finishes.
