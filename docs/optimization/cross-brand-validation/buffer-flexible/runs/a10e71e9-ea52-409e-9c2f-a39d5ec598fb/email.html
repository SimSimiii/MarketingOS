Hi there,

You run your own social, and some weeks you'd rather not do it live every day.

Buffer is social media software that holds your planned posts in a queue, in the order they'll go out — Facebook and Instagram among them.

When something happens in the shop, you add a post and drag it to the front, and the rest shift down instead of being rewritten.

That's how Foster Coffee Company works it: far enough ahead that they're not scrambling to find content, yet free to click and drag in a last-minute post when the day calls for one.

On the Free plan you line up 10 scheduled posts per channel at a time — so ten each for Facebook and Instagram — and paid plans are unlimited. The plans page confirms exactly what's included, so it's worth a look before you start a weekly queue.
