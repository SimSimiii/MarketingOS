Hi there,

You keep the shop's social going yourself — a post here, a post there, whenever a quiet minute shows up.

That works until the week gets busy. Posting one at a time is the first thing to drop when the counter's full, and the page goes quiet for a stretch.

Buffer is scheduling software that lets you line up a couple of weeks of posts for your Facebook and Instagram pages, then drop in whatever happens in the shop today.

You click the timely post into the queue, drag the rest back into order, and your plan reshuffles around it — nothing is locked to the slot you first gave it.

Foster Coffee, a two-location Michigan coffee shop, found two weeks to be their sweet spot — far enough ahead to stay ahead, close enough to add something last-minute.

Before you build a week's queue, it's worth checking the limits. The Free plan holds 10 scheduled posts per channel at once, and a slot opens back up each time a post publishes.
