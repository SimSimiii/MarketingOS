Hi there,

Plan several social posts ahead while keeping their order flexible. Buffer is a social media scheduling tool with a visible queue you can rearrange as the week changes.

Add the week’s planned posts to the queue. If changed opening hours need an earlier spot, reschedule that update by dragging it ahead of the other posts.

On the Free plan, you can connect up to three channels and schedule 10 posts per channel.

Before trying a weekly queue, check the official Free plan against the channels you use and the number of posts you want lined up. You’ll know whether its limits fit your week.
