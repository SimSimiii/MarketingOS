Subject: Plan the week, keep today open
Preview text: Queue several posts, then rearrange them when a local update takes priority.

Hi there,

Buffer is a social publishing tool that lets you line up several posts for the week without treating the plan as fixed.

Add posts to your Buffer queue. When a shop, event, or community update needs priority, drag the queued posts into a new order and give the timely update an earlier slot.

Foster Coffee uses this approach to plan two weeks ahead while still making last-minute changes by clicking and dragging posts around.

On Buffer’s Free plan, each channel can hold up to 10 scheduled posts at one time. Once a post is published, that slot becomes available again.

Before trying a weekly queue, check the official Free plan to see whether that allowance fits the number of posts you want ready.
