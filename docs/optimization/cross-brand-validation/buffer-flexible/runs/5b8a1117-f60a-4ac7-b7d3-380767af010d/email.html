Posting to Facebook and Instagram yourself means two jobs at once: lining up the week, and finding room for whatever happens at the shop today.

Buffer is social media management software for scheduling those posts. Rather than pin each one to a fixed time, you drop them into a queue you can rearrange or shuffle whenever you want.

So when something comes up at the shop, you add that post to the queue and rearrange the order — no starting the week over.

Foster Coffee works a couple of weeks ahead — far enough out to "stay ahead of the schedule without scrambling to find content," they say, yet loose enough to "just click and drag stuff around" for a last-minute change.

The rearrangeable queue is free — it's on every Buffer plan, and the Free plan holds 10 scheduled posts per channel at a time.

Worth checking before you build one: whether a week of your posts fits inside that free limit.
