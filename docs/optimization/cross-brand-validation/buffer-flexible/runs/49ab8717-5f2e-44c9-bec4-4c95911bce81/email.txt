Hi there,

You can line up a couple of weeks of Facebook and Instagram posts in Buffer, then still drop in a timely update when something happens in the shop.

Posting day by day, it's easy to fall behind when the week gets busy — and there's nothing holding the plan together when something suddenly needs to go out.

Here's how it works: Buffer runs from a queue. Set your posting times, then fill the next week or two ahead.

When something timely comes up, create that post and drop it into the next available slot — or drag it to the front. The rest of the queue simply shifts around it.

Foster Coffee, a fellow local business, plans far enough ahead to stay off the weekly scramble and still clicks and drags in last-minute changes.

Before you build a week's queue, it's worth seeing what the Free plan covers: up to 3 connected channels and 10 scheduled posts per channel at a time, with a slot freeing up each time a post publishes.
