Hi there,

With Buffer, you can line up a week of posts ahead of time and still drop in something that happened in the shop today.

Posting one at a time forces a trade-off. Plan nothing and you scramble for something to post; commit to a fixed run and it's awkward to interrupt when a timely local moment comes up.

Buffer is social media scheduling software for queuing Facebook and Instagram posts ahead of time. Your posts sit in a queue in the order they'll go out, and you rearrange them by clicking and dragging.

So when something happens today, you drag that post to the top and push the rest back — a drag, not a rebuild. It's the plan-ahead-yet-flexible way Foster Coffee works.

Before you build a weekly queue, it's worth checking the Free plan. It holds 10 scheduled posts per channel at one time; once a post publishes, the slot opens again.

Take a look and see whether that fits the week you have in mind.
