Ask the question on the page

Hi there,

Ask one focused product question at the point where someone is using the feature it concerns. They can respond there, without being sent to a separate form page.

Tally is an online form builder that lets you create the form first, then render it inline on your website. To embed it, add Tally’s widget script and an iframe with `data-tally-src` to the page.

Start with one question on one relevant page. It is a practical way to see how Tally fits your product-feedback loop before deciding where else a form would be useful.

You can create unlimited forms and submissions free while your use stays within Tally’s fair usage guidelines.

Create a free form → https://tally.so/create
