Bring order context into every conversation

Hi there,

Pull up a shopper’s order status, shipping details, or tracking information while their conversation remains open.

Crisp brings Shopify customer and order context into a shared Inbox for operators. Hugo AI Agent can use selected Shopify tools to retrieve that information or a customer’s default address during order-related conversations.

Setup stays under your control. Install the Crisp Shopify app, then separately connect Shopify in Hugo’s AI Agent settings. Enable each tool individually, adding required parameters and identity verification where applicable.

Crisp’s Shopify integration guide shows the available operations and required configuration so you can decide whether they fit your support workflow.

Review the Shopify integration → https://help.crisp.chat/en/article/how-to-install-crisp-live-chat-on-shopify-stores-and-connect-hugo-ai-agent-1ee520y/
