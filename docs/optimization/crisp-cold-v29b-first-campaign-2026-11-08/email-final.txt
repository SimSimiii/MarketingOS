Bring order context into every conversation

Hi there,

Retrieve an order’s status, shipping details, and tracking information while the customer conversation is still open.

Crisp combines storefront chat, a shared Inbox, and Hugo AI Agent. Once the Crisp Shopify app is connected, operators can access available customer and order context in the Inbox, while Hugo can use selected Shopify tools for order questions alongside answers drawn from your own support content.

You control those operations individually. After separately connecting Shopify in Hugo’s AI Agent settings, enable only the tools you want—such as Get Order Details or Get Customer Address—and configure their required parameters and identity verification where applicable.

Crisp’s Shopify integration guide documents the available context, each Hugo tool, and the configuration required. See exactly what your team and AI Agent could handle before deciding whether Crisp fits your support workflow.
