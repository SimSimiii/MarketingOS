For a client project with a defined horizon, plans can be mapped at three levels: the month ahead, the coming week and today. Papier’s Your Plans on Paper is a softback spiral productivity planner that brings those views together across 16 weeks.

Its monthly, weekly and daily planning pages are undated. That gives you one bounded paper structure to assess against the shape of a focused client project, without tying the plan to fixed calendar dates.

If that scope fits the project, the link opens the Your Plans on Paper personalisation page, where you can personalise the planner and add it to your basket.
