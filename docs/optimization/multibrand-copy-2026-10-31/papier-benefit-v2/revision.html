Give one client project 16 weeks on paper

Hi there,

If a client project fits a focused 16-week cycle, Papier’s Your Plans on Paper productivity planner gives you 16 weeks of undated pages to organise it on paper.

Use the goal breakdowns to divide the project, then plan the work across monthly, weekly and daily pages. The same project stays in view at three useful distances, without turning the planner into another screen.

Flick through the planner on the product page to inspect those layouts and decide whether its structure fits the way you would organise your client project.
