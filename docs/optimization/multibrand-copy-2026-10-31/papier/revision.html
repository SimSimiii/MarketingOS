A client project can take shape on paper across 16 undated weeks.

Papier’s Your Plans on Paper is a softback spiral productivity planner with goal breakdowns and monthly, weekly and daily planning pages. The pages are undated, so the 16-week frame is not tied to printed calendar dates.

That gives you several horizons to inspect: the project’s wider arc, the shape of a week and the work planned for a given day.

Open the product page’s Look inside view to flick through those formats and decide whether this paper structure suits the way you want to organise the project.
