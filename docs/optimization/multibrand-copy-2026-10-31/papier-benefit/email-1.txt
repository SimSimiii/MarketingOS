Give one client project a place on paper

Hi there,

For a substantial client project, a defined paper planning cycle can make the full stretch of work easier to see—without tying it to fixed calendar dates.

Papier’s Your Plans on Paper is a softback spiral productivity planner with 16 weeks of undated pages. It combines clear goal breakdowns with monthly, weekly and daily planning, giving the overall goal and the work beneath it one bounded place on paper.

Because the pages are undated, the planning cycle can begin when the project does. The structure stays specific: one 16-week span, viewed from the monthly picture down to the day’s work.

The product page lets you flick through the planner and inspect those monthly, weekly and daily pages. See whether the structure fits the way you want to organise one client project.
