Subject: A customer view of repair stages
Preview text: See how a read-only status link works on a shared board.

Hi there,

If you are considering whether customers should be able to inspect repair stages, the useful first question is what that view would actually show.

Repairboard is a shared repair-status board. It displays each repair’s current stage, and a shop can share a customer-facing link to that status.

The customer link is read-only. Customers can inspect the repair stage, but they cannot use the link to change it.

The sample board shows how Repairboard presents current repair stages, so you can judge whether this approach is relevant enough to evaluate further for your shop.
