Hi there,

Customers can inspect where a repair stands without being able to change its status.

Repairboard is a shared repair-status board that displays each repair’s current stage. A shop can share a read-only status link with its customer, so the customer sees the status without editing the board.

The sample shows this presentation directly: the shared board, the repair stages, and the customer-facing view. You can inspect how the information is arranged and decide whether this way of presenting repair status is relevant to your shop.

That gives you something concrete to assess before considering anything further.
