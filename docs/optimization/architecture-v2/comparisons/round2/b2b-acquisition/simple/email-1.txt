Subject: A shared view of repair stages
Preview text: See how customers could inspect a repair’s current stage.

Hi there,

Repairboard is a shared repair-status board that displays each repair’s current stage.

A shop can also give a customer a read-only status link. That gives the customer a way to inspect the stage without being able to change the board.

The sample shows the board directly, so you can judge whether this is a useful way to present repair progress to your customers.

Take a look at how the stages appear and whether the customer-facing view provides the right level of information for your shop.
