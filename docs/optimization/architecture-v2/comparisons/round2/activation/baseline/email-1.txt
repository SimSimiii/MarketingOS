Subject: Connect your drive, then search
Preview text: Indexing runs in the background, and search starts after the first folder is ready.

Hi there,

Your first drive connection puts Portway to work.

Portway is a searchable workspace for files in a connected shared drive. Once you connect a drive, Portway begins indexing it in the background.

Search becomes available as soon as the first folder finishes indexing. You can begin searching at that point while Portway continues indexing beyond that folder.

The connection starts the process; it does not make the entire drive searchable immediately. There is no need to wait for the whole drive to finish before using search.

Start the connection when you are ready for the first folder to begin indexing.
