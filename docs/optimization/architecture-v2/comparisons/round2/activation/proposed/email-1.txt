Subject: Connect your first drive
Preview text: Background indexing starts after connection, and search follows the first indexed folder.

Hi there,

Portway is a searchable workspace for files in a shared drive. To start making those files available for search, the first step is connecting a drive.

Once connected, Portway indexes the drive in the background. You do not need to wait for the entire drive to finish indexing before trying search.

Search becomes available as soon as the first folder finishes indexing. That gives you a clear point to check whether your first available search is ready to use.

Open the connection page to connect your first drive and begin background indexing. After the first folder finishes, you can start searching the files indexed so far.
