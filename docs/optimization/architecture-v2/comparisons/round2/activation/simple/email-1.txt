Hi there,

Your first useful step in Portway is connecting a drive.

Portway is a searchable workspace for shared-drive files. Once a drive is connected, Portway indexes it in the background.

You do not need to wait for the whole drive to finish indexing before trying search. Search becomes available as soon as the first folder finishes indexing.

That means indexing can still be running while search is already available. The point to watch is the first folder finishing, rather than the entire drive reaching the end.

Connect your first drive to start background indexing. Search will follow when that first folder is finished.
