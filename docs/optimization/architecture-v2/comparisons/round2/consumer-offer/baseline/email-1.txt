Hi there,

ClayRoom’s beginner pottery workshop is a chance to try hand building with clay during a creative weekend activity.

The clay and tools are provided, and an instructor leads the introduction. You’ll shape the clay by hand rather than use a pottery wheel, so you can understand the format before deciding whether it appeals.

After the workshop, the pieces are fired and collected when finished.

The programme gives you a closer look at what the workshop involves, so you can decide whether hand building is something you’d like to explore.
