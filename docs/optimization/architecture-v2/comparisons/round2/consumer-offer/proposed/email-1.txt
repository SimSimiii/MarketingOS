BEGINNER WORKSHOP

Try pottery through hand building

Hi there,

ClayRoom’s beginner pottery workshop is an instructor-led introduction to hand building. You’ll work with clay using the tools provided as you explore this approach to pottery.

This is not a pottery-wheel session. The workshop centres on shaping clay by hand, so you can picture the experience accurately before deciding whether it interests you.

After firing, finished pieces are collected from ClayRoom.

The programme lets you inspect the workshop format and decide whether this is a creative weekend activity you’d like to explore further.
