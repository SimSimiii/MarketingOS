POTTERY WORKSHOP

Try hand building with clay

Hi there,

ClayRoom’s beginner pottery workshop is a chance to try hand building with guidance from an instructor. Clay and tools are included, so you can focus on working with the material and following the introduction.

This is a hand-building workshop rather than a pottery wheel session. Once the finished pieces have been fired, they are collected afterwards.

If you’re curious about shaping clay by hand, the programme gives you the next place to look. Inspect it to see what the beginner workshop covers and decide if it sounds like a creative activity you’d enjoy trying.
