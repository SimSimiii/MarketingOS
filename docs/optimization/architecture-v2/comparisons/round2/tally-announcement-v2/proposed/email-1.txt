Tally is available: an online form builder that can make a submitted form the starting point for an automated client workflow.

For a custom route, a Tally webhook sends every submission as structured JSON to an HTTP endpoint in real time. That gives the workflow a defined input—not just a form collecting answers.

For no-code routing, Tally documents connections through Make, Zapier, and n8n. Those platforms can pass submission data onward to CRMs, billing tools, internal databases, or messaging apps.

Create one free form and evaluate it as the intake layer for a client route you’re considering. You’ll have a concrete source of submission data to check against the destination and field mapping that workflow requires.
