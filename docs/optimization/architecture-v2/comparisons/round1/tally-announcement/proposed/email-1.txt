Hi there,

Meet Tally, an online form builder that can turn each new submission into the starting signal for an automated workflow.

With a webhook configured, Tally can send the response data as structured JSON to any HTTP endpoint in real time. Your endpoint can then begin the workflow you need.

That could mean your own backend or a flow routed through Make, Zapier or n8n to a CRM, billing tool, internal database or messaging app. You’re not limited to one preset destination.

Tally can also sign webhook requests with a SHA256 signature, so your receiving system can verify where the payload came from before processing it.

One form is enough to assess whether Tally fits as the intake point for your next workflow.
