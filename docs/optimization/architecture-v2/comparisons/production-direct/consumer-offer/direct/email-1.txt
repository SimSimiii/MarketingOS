POTTERY WORKSHOP

Spend a weekend trying hand building

Hi there,

ClayRoom’s beginner pottery workshop is a chance to work with clay and try hand building under an instructor’s guidance.

Clay and tools are included, so the session can focus on shaping pieces by hand. This workshop does not include a pottery wheel session.

Your finished pieces will be fired and available to collect afterwards. If that sounds like a creative way to spend part of your weekend, inspect the programme to see what the workshop covers.
