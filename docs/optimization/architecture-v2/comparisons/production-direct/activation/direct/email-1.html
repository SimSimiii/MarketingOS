Hi there,

Your searchable workspace starts with a connected drive.

Portway is a searchable workspace for shared-drive files. When you connect a drive, Portway begins indexing it in the background so its contents can be searched.

You do not need to wait for the entire drive to finish indexing. Search becomes available as soon as the first folder finishes, while indexing continues in the background.

The connection page lets you add your first drive. Once it is connected, the first folder to finish indexing marks the point when you can begin searching its files.
