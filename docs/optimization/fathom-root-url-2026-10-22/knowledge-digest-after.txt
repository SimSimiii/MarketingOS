# Script installation
To start collecting data from your website, you need to [install our script](/docs/script/embed) in the header of your site’s code via our [WordPress plugin](/docs/integrations/wordpress) or using [one of our integrations](/docs/integrations)).

Fathom’s dashboard is live and real-time, so the best way to check if our script is installed is by visiting your website and seeing if your visit shows up in the “Realtime” count at the top of your dashboard.

If you believe you’ve got our script installed on your website and you are either not seeing any data or seeing incorrect data, investigate the following: [If Fathom isn’t working on your site](/docs/troubleshooting/not-working).

# EU isolation
If you want to use [EU isolation](https://usefathom.com/features/data-isolation), congratulations; as a Fathom customer, you’re already using it, and it’s enabled by default!

There’s nothing you have to set up or tweak or even change in [your embed script](/docs/script/embed) to use it. Fathom automatically and intelligently routes data from EU visitors to be processed in the EU and all other visitors to be processed in the US.

EU isolation provides compliance and speed in loading your script and processing the data.

It includes visitors from the following non-eu member nations:

- Iceland
- Liechtenstein
- Norway
- Switzerland
- United Kingdom

### [Our EU server locations](#servers)
- Falkenstein
- Helsinki
- Nuremberg

### [Extreme EU Isolation](#extreme)
If you need to use Extreme EU Isolation, where all global traffic is routed through the EU, please use `https://cdn-eu.usefathom.com/script.js` instead.

# Keep EU visitors’ personal data in the EU
Lots of analytics software claims to comply with various privacy laws, such as GDPR, PECR or the ePrivacy Directive, but most of them are putting you and your website visitors at risk.

Data Isolation was built in collaboration with EU legal experts, and is part of our commitment to simplify your compliance.

EU visitors’ personal data stays in the EU. Anonymized analytics are stored on our US infrastructure. Privacy laws will keep changing, and we want to process data in the country it belongs. The EU and the US are the two biggest markets for that today. This feature exists for all customers on all plans and happens without any programming or setup required.
