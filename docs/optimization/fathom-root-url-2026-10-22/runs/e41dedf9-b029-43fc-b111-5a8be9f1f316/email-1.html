You can inspect Fathom Analytics’ live dashboard before changing anything on your site. It lets you judge the whole one-page view on its own terms.

Fathom Analytics is website analytics software that puts live visitors, pageviews, top pages, referrers, countries, devices, browsers, events, and UTM campaigns on a single dashboard page. Once its script is installed, the dashboard updates in real time.

If a basic traffic answer currently sends you through layers of reports, this is the useful test: can one page show the information you actually want to check?

The live demo lets you click through that presentation before installing Fathom or deciding to test it with your own traffic. You can inspect the interface first and make that call.
