# MarketingOS pilot — Mehdi

Use the platform in English. Your account has 50 monthly executions shared by campaign generation, knowledge compilation and research jobs. This is an execution quota, not a dollar spending cap.

## Try three different offers

Choose offers you understand well: one physical/e-commerce product, one SaaS or digital service, and one affiliate or other offer. Start with a single English email for each offer before requesting a longer sequence. Bring the real product page and any important pricing, conditions, exclusions or approved affiliate claims. Do not upload customer lists.

For each offer:

1. Register a brand and add its source material. Notice whether you understand what to add and when compilation is needed.
2. Run competitor analysis and audience mapping if they are useful for that offer. Check whether the named alternatives really compete and whether cited pages support the conclusions.
3. Request one email for a specific audience and objective. Use a real destination link. Start with Balanced review depth.
4. During generation, note the wait and whether progress is clear. Use Refresh to see the latest saved status.
5. Check every number, price, restriction, product claim and link. Compare the result with your own writing process or usual assistant.
6. Copy/export the draft into your normal editing tool, make the necessary edits, and record how long they take. Keep this as a draft during the pilot.
7. For your best case, request a two- or three-email sequence. If useful, duplicate the same brief and compare Fast with Balanced; keep the source material and audience unchanged.

Treat an absent competitor claim as unknown, rather than proof that the competitor lacks the feature. Audience descriptions are research hypotheses, not facts about every recipient. If generation asks for missing material, check whether your source actually documents the promised capability or condition. Do not fill the gap with an invented price, example, testimonial or timing estimate.
