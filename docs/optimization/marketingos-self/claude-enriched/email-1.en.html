Your account is already open. Signing in puts you in a workspace where you register your brand, add the real material for one launch or onboarding offer, and ask for the emails you need in your own words.

MarketingOS hands back reviewed draft text — subject, preview, body, call to action — for you to check and copy into whatever you send with. It drafts; it does not send.

Five onboarding emails out of a chat model in an afternoon is a fair comparison to make. The difference is in method, not in adjectives.

Your material is read into a set of facts the writing is allowed to claim, so each sentence traces back to something you supplied.

Then every draft is cold-read by a simulated reader who has never seen the brief, and what did not land is rewritten before it reaches you. That pass has no equivalent in a blank prompt.

None of it exists until the setup is done: a new account has no campaign waiting, so the brand and the real material go in first, then one request.
