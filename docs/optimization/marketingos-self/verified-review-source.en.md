# MarketingOS review features — verified local product observations, 15 October 2026

MarketingOS uses AI models to draft, cold-read and critique emails. The cold reader is an AI simulation that has not seen the brief, not a human reviewer. Its assessments are not measured customer opens, clicks or sales.

The brand's Knowledge page lets users inspect compiled facts, supporting source quotations, evidence identifiers, strength labels and missing information. The completed campaign's execution page displays the draft and a review report. In "Why it scored what it scored", users can inspect what the simulated reader understood, where they stopped, their biggest doubt and suggested changes. These were opened and checked in the local application on 15 October 2026. The knowledge page and the execution report are separate pages within the workspace.

Existing invited users sign in at https://app.orqagent.com/login/. Login is a gateway to their workspace; it does not directly open a pre-existing draft or report. A new account has no generated campaign waiting. If the user has not run a campaign, they first register a brand, add real source material and request an email. After a run produces a draft, they can review its text and reader report, and compare its claims with the compiled knowledge and original source material. If they already have a result, they can open that result to review it.

No typical hands-on setup time has been measured. Do not promise that preparation is quick, takes a stated number of minutes or saves a measured amount of time. Generation may take several minutes and can finish with partial delivery or need additional material. Users must check facts, links and brand voice before copying drafts into their own sending tool. No public signup, price, testimonial or conversion guarantee is established.
