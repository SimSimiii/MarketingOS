A writer and editor can work on the same newsletter without sharing one broad role.

Buttondown is email newsletter software with configurable teammate permissions. An owner can grant Read & write access to email management—which covers viewing, editing, and creating emails—while withholding the separate permission to send emails to subscribers.

Other permission areas can be configured as None, Read, or Read & write. Sending is handled separately, so the person editing an issue does not automatically need authority to send it.

Teams is available on the Professional plan. The official Teams documentation lists the permission areas and their configurations, letting you check the available boundaries against the writing, editing, and sending split you need.
