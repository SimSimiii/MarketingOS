Before you open signup, Loops can give your Supabase auth and onboarding emails one place to live.

Use Supabase’s Send Email Hook to send confirmation, magic-link, password-reset, reauthentication, and security emails through Loops. Each auth action type can have its own template.

Then add the separate Supabase contact sync to turn new `auth.users` records into Loops contacts and trigger a welcome or onboarding workflow.

The Supabase guide covers the required Edge Function, template mapping, contact sync, and sending-domain setup so you can evaluate the full path before launch.
