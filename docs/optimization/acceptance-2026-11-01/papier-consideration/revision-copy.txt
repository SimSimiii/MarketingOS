Plan a client project across 16 weeks

Hello,

Sixteen weeks is long enough for a substantial client project and short enough to keep in view. On paper, the whole run can sit in one place.

Papier's Your Plans on Paper Softback Spiral Productivity Planner gives that run a bounded structure: 16 weeks of undated pages, supported by goal breakdowns. Because nothing is pre-dated, you decide where week one falls.

From there, monthly, weekly and daily planning pages organise the work at three cadences. The product page's Look inside view shows the goal-planning space and those layouts, so you can judge whether they suit your client-project workflow.
