Hi there,

Turn Intercom support conversations into organized product feedback. Once Intercom is connected to Canny, Autopilot reads support tickets and pulls out the feature requests inside them.

Canny is customer feedback management software that brings requests from customer conversations into one place. Instead of leaving the product signal inside a ticket, Autopilot captures it as feedback your product team can use.

Autopilot then merges detected duplicates. Repeated requests are consolidated before the feedback is triaged to the appropriate team.

The result is a clear flow from the original support conversation to captured, deduplicated, and triaged feedback in Canny—ready for your team to review when making product decisions.
