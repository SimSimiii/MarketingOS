## Bannerbear — crédits

**Subject** Plan your Bannerbear test: understand image credits before you render  
**Preview** One JPG costs one credit. Adding PNG doubles the cost for the same design.

Hi there,

Planning your Bannerbear trial test? Before generating your seasonal product banners, it's worth understanding how image credits affect your budget.

Here's the difference between one format and two:

- Single JPG: One credit at scale 1.
- JPG + PNG: Two credits at scale 1, because each requested format counts separately.

The same principle applies when you increase the image scale. For example:

- JPG at scale 2: 2 credits.
- JPG + PNG at scale 2: 4 credits.

In other words, credits are calculated per format and multiplied by the requested scale.

Our recommendation: Start your test with one JPG at scale 1. Once you've evaluated the design, decide whether you also need PNG versions for your campaign.

This gives you a straightforward baseline for estimating the credit cost before expanding your test.

Your next step: Review Bannerbear's pricing and credit calculations before generating your test images.
