Subject: Turn blog posts into email
Preview text: Let your RSS feed supply each subscriber email.

Hi there,

Keep publishing on your site. Buttondown is an email newsletter platform that can turn new RSS feed items into subscriber emails.

Build one template around your feed and choose the per-new-item cadence. Buttondown then checks the feed every thirty minutes, and a new item can trigger an email without separate per-post composition.

RSS-to-email is a $9-per-month add-on to the base offering. Buttondown’s official documentation shows how feed fields populate the template and how the per-item cadence works, so you can decide whether the workflow fits your blog.

See the RSS workflow → https://docs.buttondown.com/rss-to-email
