Publish on your site. Send from the feed.

Hi there,

Your blog can remain where the writing begins. With Buttondown, its RSS feed can also supply the email your subscribers receive.

Buttondown is an email newsletter platform with RSS-to-email automation. Build one template around your feed, choose the per-new-item cadence, and a newly published item can trigger an email without a separate round of newsletter composition.

Buttondown checks the feed every thirty minutes, so the trigger follows that interval rather than firing instantly. RSS-to-email is a $9-per-month add-on to the base offering.

The official Buttondown documentation shows how feed fields populate the template and how the per-item cadence works. Review the mechanism, then decide whether it fits the way you publish.

See the RSS workflow → https://docs.buttondown.com/rss-to-email
