Hi there,

A score alone cannot tell you whether a draft deserves your trust.

MarketingOS is an email campaign drafting and market-research application that lets you examine the basis of a completed campaign. Its Knowledge page shows the compiled facts, supporting source quotations, evidence identifiers, strength labels, and missing information behind the draft.

The “Why it scored what it scored” report adds a different view: what the simulated reader understood, where they stopped, their biggest doubt, and the changes suggested after the cold-read.

That assessment is not evidence of how actual recipients will open, click, or buy. It gives you specific observations to judge alongside your own material—not a performance claim to accept on faith.

Open the finished campaign in your existing workspace and compare both views with the draft. You can then decide which language is usable, which claims need checking, and what should be rewritten before the copy goes anywhere else.
