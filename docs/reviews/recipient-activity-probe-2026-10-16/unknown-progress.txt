Hi there,

An AI opinion does not tell you how real recipients will respond. MarketingOS makes the review useful in a narrower way: it puts the factual basis and the cold-read diagnosis where you can inspect them.

On the Knowledge page, you can see the compiled facts, supporting source quotations, evidence identifiers, strength labels, and information the source material did not establish.

In “Why it scored what it scored,” the report shows what the simulated reader understood, where they stopped, their biggest doubt, and suggested changes. Those observations help you decide which language to keep, check, or rewrite.

They are not measured opens, clicks, or sales. If you have a completed campaign, inspect both views before using the draft; if you do not, the same workspace lets you begin a campaign first.
